## Reproduction and scope

Before editing production code, a generated fixture loaded two supported Codex
0.158.0 envelopes, rewrote one as a summary-only file and removed the other. Main
retained both rows and reported a load error. An empty Claude root disappeared
after its first scan without reporting an error. A summary-only Claude file also
produced a blocking error. This demonstrates scanner behavior with synthetic data,
not observed customer impact.

The scanner now reports format uncertainty separately from read uncertainty.
Unsupported, ambiguous or partial files keep only their own cached identity, and
independently verified deletions can proceed. Grok folders without a usable summary
produce a notice and protect metadata for that folder. A scan with an unreadable
root or subdirectory keeps cached records and reports `loadError`. The store tracks
roots previously observed during its lifetime, including roots with no sessions.
Unknown coverage cannot clear earlier notices or authorize metadata housekeeping.

The conversation list contains an accessible, wrapping notice that scrolls with
history; Settings contains its provider details. Saved project pins and focus stay
until absence can be verified. Native metadata retains its prior conservative
policy; notes are not pruned. Seeding records verified chats immediately and keeps
its migration pending until history coverage is complete. Existing error guards
remain intact. Every applied scan publishes a sequence change so equal notice
counts cannot hide a change to protected folders from housekeeping.
The six-provider Settings inventory remains REA-1261.
