The baseline direct production-source harness reproduced a lost rename at byte
717,045 in a 923,107-byte transcript. A further 1,049,570 generated bytes left
the row on its first prompt. A small transcript accepted the identical rename.

The implementation streams Claude records and caches bounded display fields,
record framing progress and a full-prefix SHA-256 fingerprint. Empty renames
restore the first prompt. Complete final JSON without a newline remains compatible
without prematurely committing its line. Oversized records discard through the
newline; their notice survives an unchanged cache hit. Interrupted/read-racing
scans do not publish partial cache progress or clear uncertain coverage.

The initial `2e3d124` candidate used fixed continuity anchors. Its 19 focused cases
passed and its smoke measured 1,049,570 new bytes plus 8,407 validation bytes.
That was insufficient: a generated rewrite inserted a newer title at byte 799,497
outside unchanged anchors, then grew the file. The warm result stayed Older title;
a forced cold scan found Unanchored rewritten rename. The counterexample receipt
and the original requirement for append-only read I/O remain in the Linear record.

On 2026-10-11 the parent explicitly rejected sampled continuity and authorized
bounded, cancellable full old-prefix revalidation on changed files. The revised
tradeoff reads every previously scanned byte in 64 KiB chunks and compares its
fingerprint before reusing parse state. A mismatch cold-parses the current file;
a match parses only appended records. Unchanged-file cache hits remain zero-read.
Changed-file validation I/O is deliberately proportional to the old prefix: the
candidate does not claim append-only byte I/O or silently retain the old requirement.
